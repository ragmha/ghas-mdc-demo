# Community code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Assume good intent, focus feedback
on the work, and help make security learning accessible to people with different
backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing another person's private
information, and disruptive or abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, demos,
workshops, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Report
conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the organization's
published contact channel. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as
practical.
